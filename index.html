Case study 02 · Agentic systems

Agentic Curriculum Engine

Agentic systems designer

Challenge

Building full curricula by hand (objectives, scripts, storyboards, knowledge checks) could not keep pace with the release cadence of new tools, services and enablement messaging, and SME accuracy had to hold.

Approach

A script agent and a storyboard agent work from the same brief in parallel, iterating inside a shared HTML design system built for fast visual review. SME validation gates and dynamic knowledge checks tied to each objective keep every release accurate.

Impact

Content stays current and tied to the exact tasks learners perform, which builds trust in unfamiliar tools. Every objective, scenario and knowledge check is stored as a tagged, searchable unit, so new projects reuse proven content.

Case study 03 · AI innovation

AI-Assisted Multimodal Platform

AI innovation lead

Approach

I built a generative pipeline. Adobe Firefly and Blockade Labs generated scene concepts, Synthesia produced the on-screen English presenter and narration, and Storyline 360 assembled the course.

Localization

The four-language rollout went through our localization vendor with established glossaries. At the time, built-in AI translation sat below a 40% fidelity bar and no validation workflow existed to rely on it safely.

Engagement

AI-avatar and human presenters showed statistically comparable engagement, a significant result given audience skepticism of AI avatars.

Case study 06 · Foundational strategy

AWS Way of Selling

Lead learning experience designer

Challenge

AWS needed to enable its global field professionals on a unified customer-engagement approach, and no scalable framework existed.

Approach

I reframed the brief to ask how we might enable rapid mastery, prioritized video, and piloted the program with iterative stakeholder feedback loops. An AI-assisted workflow built the curriculum in 3 weeks instead of 3 months.

Impact

Sellers saw an 8.9% higher win rate and a 36.3% shorter sales cycle. The framework was validated for expansion to 50,000+ learners.
